1. Introduction

CSD Good Services LLC ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our landing page and submit your information to request a quote or contact us.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide you with service quotes and to optimize our advertising campaigns:

  • Information you provide to us: When you fill out our contact form, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and details about your property (property type, square footage, cleaning frequency).
  • Automated Tracking & Cookies: Because this website is used for digital advertising campaigns (like Google Ads), we automatically collect data regarding your interaction with our site. This includes IP addresses, browser types, interaction data, and tracking identifiers (such as Google Click IDs - GCLID, UTM parameters).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect strictly for the following purposes:

  • To contact you (via phone, text, or email) to provide the cleaning or maintenance quote you requested.
  • To process and manage your inquiry through our CRM system.
  • To analyze website behavior and optimize our digital advertising campaigns (e.g., tracking which ads lead to successful form submissions).

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We only share your data with trusted service providers necessary to operate our business, such as:

  • Our CRM software provider (GoHighLevel) to manage your quote request.
  • Analytics and advertising platforms (Google Analytics, Google Ads) to measure campaign performance.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access or disclosure. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you or ask us to delete it. If you wish to opt out of future communications after submitting a form, you can do so by replying "STOP" to our messages or contacting us directly.
